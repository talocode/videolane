# Short-Form Playbook

Use this as the default production standard for new Talocode and Tera vertical videos.

## Non-negotiables

- Match voiceover to the full video duration. Do not leave narrated content followed by silent filler.
- Make the first spoken and on-screen line understandable without prior context.
- Show the real product, workflow, or learning action when making a product claim.
- Use only claims that can be demonstrated or measured in the video.
- Build one idea per video. Do not combine multiple unrelated tips or features.

## Hook Structure

Start with a familiar problem, reveal the missing insight, then give a concrete action.

```text
[Known pain] + [unknown cause or better path] + [specific action]
```

Example:

```text
Studied for hours but remember nothing?
Rereading hides what you cannot recall.
Close your notes and answer three questions from memory.
```

For a product demo, use a truthful hook stack:

```text
[Demonstrable outcome] + [simple mechanism] + [open question] + [screen evidence]
```

Do not use unsupported revenue, time-saving, performance, or popularity claims to create the hook.

## Approved Formats

### Active Recall

Use for Tera study content. Start with a study pain, explain why passive review fails, then demonstrate one recall action.

Template: `templates/tera-active-recall-short`

### Exam Countdown

Use for time-bound study anxiety. State the deadline, prioritize the work, then give a small study plan.

Template: `templates/tera-exam-countdown-short`

### Study Conversation

Use a two-person exchange to resolve one common study objection. Keep the dialogue natural and make the final line actionable.

Template: `templates/tera-study-conversation-short`

### Proof Demo

Use for Talocode workflows. Show a real input, the exact action, and the finished result. Keep the proof sequence uncut where practical.

Template: `templates/talocode-proof-demo-short`

### Mistake and Fix

Show one fragile workflow step, explain its consequence, and demonstrate the reliable replacement.

Template: `templates/talocode-mistake-short`

### Reaction Proof

Open with a genuine reaction, then switch immediately to a timed screen demonstration. The reaction is the hook; the product proof earns trust.

Template: `templates/reaction-proof-short`

### Founder Lesson

Share a specific decision, what it changed, and a lesson the viewer can apply. Avoid vague motivation.

Template: `templates/founder-lesson-short`

## Launch Content

For a release video or announcement:

1. Lead with the capability, not the backstory.
2. Show the first command or first action immediately.
3. Demonstrate one complete, useful result.
4. State whether the release is early and invite focused feedback.
5. Put source, documentation, and the next action in the first follow-up post or description.

## Pre-Publish Check

- The first two seconds contain the hook on screen and in voiceover.
- The video demonstrates the claim rather than merely describing it.
- Every scene advances the same single idea.
- Captions are readable on a mobile screen.
- The CTA tells the viewer one exact next action.
- The exported video includes voiceover through its final frame.
